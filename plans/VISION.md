# G8aural: ABRSM Aural Training Application

## Educational Purpose

G8aural is a web-based aural training application designed specifically for students preparing for ABRSM (Associated Board of the Royal Schools of Music) examinations. The application focuses on two critical components of aural tests:

1. **Cadence Identification**: Students listen to chord progressions and identify cadence types (Perfect, Plagal, Imperfect, Interrupted)
2. **Voice Singing**: Students sing back melodies from memory after hearing them played

## Core Features

### Cadence Identification
- Students listen to progressions establishing key and ending with a target cadence
- Four cadence types (Perfect, Plagal, Imperfect, Interrupted)
- Grade-specific difficulty levels (6-8)
- Musical-quality progressions using Bach corpus patterns
- Interactive feedback with visual notation after correct answers

### Voice Singing
- Grade-specific melodic exercises (5-8)
- Real-time pitch detection and analysis
- Octave-invariant grading system
- Visual feedback with pitch contour display
- Adaptive to student vocal range

## ABRSM Syllabus Alignment

### Grade 5
- **Voice Singing**: Single melody to sing from memory

### Grade 6
- **Cadence Types**: Perfect, Imperfect (2 types)
- **Voice Singing**: Sing the upper part of a two-part phrase

### Grade 7
- **Cadence Types**: Perfect, Imperfect, Interrupted (3 types)
- **Voice Singing**: Sing the lower part of a two-part phrase

### Grade 8
- **Cadence Types**: Perfect, Plagal, Imperfect, Interrupted (all 4 types)
- **Voice Singing**: Sing the lowest part of a three-part phrase

## Educational Value

- **Immediate Feedback**: Students receive instant feedback on their performance
- **Independent Practice**: Allows for self-directed learning outside lessons
- **Musical Quality**: Bach corpus patterns ensure authentic musical examples
- **Grade Progression**: Students can practice at their current level or prepare for the next
- **Accessible Learning**: Web-based interface requires no specialized equipment
